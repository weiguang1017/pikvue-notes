What Happens When Your LLM Provider Goes Down? Fallback Design for Indie SaaS

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

Your AI feature worked perfectly in the demo, then one Tuesday your provider returns 503s for forty minutes and every model call becomes a spinner. The uncomfortable truth for a one-person company is that you cannot control provider uptime — you can only control what your product does during the gap. This guide is about that gap: the four failure modes that actually hit small teams, the retry and fallback layers that fix them, and how to build all of it without doubling your bill.
