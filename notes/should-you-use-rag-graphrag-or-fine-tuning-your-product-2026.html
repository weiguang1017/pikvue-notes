Should You Use Rag Graphrag Or Fine Tuning Your Product 2026

When an AI must work with your own knowledge — a codebase, a manual, a product spec — you have three ways to make the model "know" it. They are retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), GraphRAG, and fine-tuning. Most teams reach for RAG by default, treat fine-tuning as the answer to everything factual, and only discover GraphRAG after RAG keeps failing on connected questions. This guide separates the three by the job they actually do, so you pick the one that fixes the pain you really have rather than the most discussed option.
