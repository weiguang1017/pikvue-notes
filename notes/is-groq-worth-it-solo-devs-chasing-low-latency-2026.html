Is Groq Worth It for Solo Devs Chasing Low Latency in 2026?

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

Latency is the feature nobody puts on the roadmap and everybody notices, and it decides whether users stay with an interface or quietly abandon it. A chat interface that starts answering in under a second feels like a different product from one that starts after three, and if you are building voice, autocomplete, or an agent that makes several model calls in sequence, per-call delay compounds into something users abandon. Groq is the provider most often recommended for that problem. Here is an honest look at where it earns its place, where it is the wrong tool, and what to check before you commit.
