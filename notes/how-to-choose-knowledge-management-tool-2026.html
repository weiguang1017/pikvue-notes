How to Choose a Knowledge Management Tool in 2026

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

Most people choose a knowledge management tool the way they choose a note-taking app: install three, use one for a week, then drift back to scattered documents and browser bookmarks. The failure is rarely discipline. It is that the tool was picked on vibes — a screenshot, a demo video, a colleague's recommendation — instead of against the specific jobs you need it to do. This guide gives you eight criteria, the order to weigh them in, and a way to test each one before you commit a year of notes to it.
