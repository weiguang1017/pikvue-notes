Headless Commerce in 2026: API-First Storefronts Explained for Indie Developers

Your SaaS sells subscriptions, but now you need a merch store and a digital-goods checkout without rebuilding your entire Next.js app around a clunky e-commerce platform. That's the headless-commerce pitch: keep the storefront and checkout platform as independent API-first pieces and you're free to design the front end your way. In 2026 the space matured — you're no longer choosing between "use Shopify templates" and "build everything." This is what the API-first landscape actually looks like and how an indie dev picks.
