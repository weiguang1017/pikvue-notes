What Is Context Engineering? A Guide for LLM Builders in 2026

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

You have probably tweaked a system prompt and watched a model's behavior shift for no obvious reason. The difference between a model that feels sharp and one that keeps drifting is often not the model at all — it is what you put into its context and how you load it. "Context engineering," the discipline of deliberately shaping what a model sees and where, has replaced a grab bag of prompt tricks as the thing that actually moves quality in 2026. It treats the context window as a scarce attention budget and makes every token earn a place.
