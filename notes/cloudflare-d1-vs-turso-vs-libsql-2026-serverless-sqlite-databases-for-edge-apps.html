Cloudflare D1 vs Turso vs LibSQL 2026: Serverless SQLite Databases for Edge Apps

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

SQLite is having a renaissance. For years it was dismissed as "just an embedded database for mobile apps," but the rise of edge computing (Vercel Edge Functions, Cloudflare Workers, Deno Deploy) has made SQLite's zero-configuration, single-file architecture ideal for distributed environments. The problem: vanilla SQLite doesn't support replication, concurrent writes, or HTTP access. In 2026, three serverless SQLite platforms have emerged to solve this: Cloudflare D1, Turso, and LibSQL.
