Do You Actually Need OpenTelemetry for Your LLM App in 2026?

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

AI applications fail in a particularly frustrating way: the prompt looks fine, the call "succeeded," and the answer is still wrong, slow, or expensive. Ordinary logs show you the request but not the steps a reasoning agent took to reach the output. In 2026 the teams that debug AI reliably are closing that gap by running LLM apps on the same observability foundation they already use for the rest of their systems, and OpenTelemetry has become the standard shared language for it. When the model call is a span in the same ledger as your database and your queue, the "mysterious answer" becomes a replayable run you can actually inspect.
