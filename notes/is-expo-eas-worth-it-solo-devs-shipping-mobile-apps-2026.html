Is Expo EAS Worth It for Solo Devs Shipping Mobile Apps in 2026?

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

Building a React Native app is the easy half; getting a signed build into TestFlight and shipping a JavaScript fix without waiting on app review is the half that eats your weekends. Expo Application Services — EAS Build, Submit, Update, and Workflows — is the managed answer to that half, and it is the default path for most Expo projects. The question worth asking is what you are paying for once the free quota runs out, and when a plain CI pipeline would serve you better.
