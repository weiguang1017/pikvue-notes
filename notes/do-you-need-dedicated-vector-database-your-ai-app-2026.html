Do You Need a Dedicated Vector Database for Your AI App in 2026?

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

Almost every AI feature that touches your own data eventually needs similarity search, and that is the moment a vendor page appears to sell you one. This guide walks through what a vector database actually does for you, when the dedicated product earns its place, and how hard it is to switch later.
