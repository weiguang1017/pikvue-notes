PostHog Review 2026: Open-Source Product Analytics for Indie SaaS Founders

A condensed summary of the full PikVue guide published 2026-11-16.

As an indie SaaS founder, your analytics choices used to be simple: start with Google Analytics because it's free, then move to Mixpanel or Amplitude when you can afford it. But in 2026, that path looks increasingly outdated. Google Analytics has become less reliable due to privacy regulations and ad blocker adoption, while traditional SaaS analytics tools charge enterprise prices for basic features. PostHog has emerged as a serious alternative — an open-source product analytics platform that competes with Mixpanel and Amplitude on features while offering a self-hosted option that never sends your user data to third parties.
